# Mira Vale

- Role: heroine.
- Age: 33.
- Occupation: historic preservation planner.
- External goal: save the Beacon House with a financially viable preservation plan.
- Wound: leaving Cedar Harbor once made her feel selfish and judged.
- False belief: if she does not control the plan, she will lose herself.
- True belief: choosing a place can be freedom when the choice is honest.
- Voice: precise, dry humor, architectural vocabulary, short when defensive.
- Growth marker: asks for help in chapter 20 instead of carrying the whole rescue alone.
