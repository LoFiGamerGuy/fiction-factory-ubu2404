# June Marlow

- Role: Beacon House owner and Mira's aunt.
- Function: emotional truth-teller and owner with real financial stakes.
- Wants: the inn to survive as a living business, not a shrine.
- Constraint: debt and storm damage make Grant's cash offer tempting.
- Continuity note: June must have agency in the final decision; Mira cannot simply save the inn for her.
