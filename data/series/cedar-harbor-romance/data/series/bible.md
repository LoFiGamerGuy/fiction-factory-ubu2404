# Cedar Harbor Romances Bible

## Series Premise

Cedar Harbor is a working waterfront town in Maine where year-round locals, seasonal money,
and fragile old buildings keep colliding. Each book should pair a concrete civic or craft problem
with a romance that can only work when both leads become more honest under pressure.

## Book 1: The Renovation Pact

Mira Vale returns to save the Beacon House, her aunt June's storm-damaged lighthouse keeper's inn.
Theo Calder, the restoration contractor Mira almost loved ten years ago, has the emergency contract.
The heritage board gives them a deadline: produce one viable restoration plan before the Solstice Walk
or the inn will likely be sold to Grant Wexler for luxury redevelopment.

## Canonical Setting Facts

- Cedar Harbor has a public pier, a heritage board, a small archive room in town hall, Lena Ortiz's cafe,
  Theo Calder's workshop, and the Beacon House on the rocky point north of the harbor.
- The Beacon House includes a lantern room, keeper's room, back stairs, damaged roof, cellar cistern,
  old chart drawer, and several guest rooms that can reopen in phases.
- The Solstice Walk is the town's early-summer tourism kickoff and the public deadline for the restoration pitch.
- Grant Wexler wants the site for a luxury facade-preservation redevelopment that would erase the inn's community use.

## Character Continuity

- Mira Vale is precise, controlled, professionally excellent, and afraid that staying in Cedar Harbor means losing herself.
- Theo Calder is tactile, plainspoken, highly competent, and prone to proving care through work instead of words.
- June Marlow owns the Beacon House and refuses to let either nostalgia or fear make the decision for her.
- Lena Ortiz owns the harbor cafe and serves as Mira's warm but unsparing friend.
- Cal Derry is Theo's crew lead and calls out Theo's avoidant habits.

## Romance Contract

- No cheating.
- Consent is explicit at every intimacy escalation.
- Theo must learn to speak before acting from fear.
- Mira must learn to ask for help before control becomes isolation.
- The HEA requires both the relationship and the Beacon House plan to be viable.
